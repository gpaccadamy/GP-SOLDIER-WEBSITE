Privacy Policy

GP Soldier Academy

Effective Date: 24 May 2026

1. Introduction

GP Soldier Academy values student privacy and is committed to protecting user information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, and used while using our website and student application.

2. Information We Collect

The following information may be collected:

  • Mobile number
  • Login credentials
  • Academic activity and test results

3. Data Storage & Security

Student information and results are stored securely in AWS database infrastructure.

Passwords are stored using hashed protection methods and are not stored in readable form.

4. Quick Notes Feature

Quick Notes created by students are stored locally on the user's device only.

These notes are not uploaded to GP Soldier Academy servers.

Quick Notes automatically delete after approximately 12 hours.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No location access
  • No camera access
  • No storage/file access
  • No payment information
  • No advertisements
  • No analytics tracking
  • No third-party tracking

6. Account Management

Student accounts are created and managed by academy administration.

Students cannot directly delete accounts. Account deactivation, restriction, or deletion may be performed by academy administration.

7. Educational Purpose

GP Soldier Academy is an educational platform intended for student learning activities including video classes, tests, results, and study support.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when required. Updated versions will be published on the official website.
